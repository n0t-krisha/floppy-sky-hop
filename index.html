💨

🖱️

Look around

Platforms & obstacles

☁️

Cloud platform

Land or walk onto solid clouds. Step off an edge and you’ll fall.

🪞

Glass bridge

One cloud in each pair is solid; the two look exactly alike.

🌫️

Fading cloud

It blinks for 2.5 seconds, disappears briefly, then returns.

🍯

Honey puddle

Sticky paws slow Bunny down for a few seconds.

🧶

Rolling yarn

A tumble briefly stuns Bunny. Time a jump to avoid it.

🌬️

Wind gust

Gusts push Bunny sideways while Bunny is in the air.

🌸

Bounce flower

Step on one for a springy, extra-high launch.

🪨

Cloud boulder

Rolling boulders bump Bunny. Jump over them.

↔️

Moving cloud

Ride the shuttle and lift clouds, then leap to the still landing clouds.
